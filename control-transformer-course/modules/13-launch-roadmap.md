# Module 13 — 90-Day Launch Roadmap

A practical checklist from decision to first commercial deliveries
(Level A start). Timing assumes premises are available on rent.

## Days 1–15 — Foundation

- [ ] Finish modules 01–03; decide initial range (suggested: 50, 100, 250,
      500, 1000 VA; primaries 230 V and 0-380-415-440 V; secondaries 24 V
      and 110 V)
- [ ] Market check: interview 15–20 panel builders — models, volumes, prices,
      pain points
- [ ] Choose business entity; open bank account; apply for MSME / GST and
      other registrations (Module 07)
- [ ] Draft project report and approach bank (Module 11)
- [ ] Shortlist premises and check power availability

## Days 16–45 — Setup & design

- [ ] Order winding machines, oven, varnish tank, test equipment
      (3 quotes each)
- [ ] Electrical installation, earthing, HV test booth
- [ ] Finalise designs with `ct_calc.py`; create design sheets
- [ ] Source laminations, wire, bobbins, insulation, varnish, terminals —
      two suppliers each; incoming-inspection plan
- [ ] Build **3 prototypes per model**; run routine + temperature-rise +
      inrush tests; adjust turns / regulation compensation
- [ ] Hire and train 3–5 operators; write work instructions
- [ ] Brand name, logo, trademark application

## Days 46–75 — Pilot production

- [ ] Pilot batch (e.g. 20 units per model); check first-pass yield
- [ ] Finalise cost sheets and price list (Module 09)
- [ ] Print catalogue; launch website and B2B listings
- [ ] Send samples to 10 target panel builders / 3 OEMs
- [ ] Book type tests at an accredited lab for the main models
- [ ] Start ISO 9001 documentation

## Days 76–90 — Commercial launch

- [ ] First commercial orders; deliver within promised time
- [ ] Appoint first distributor
- [ ] Set up CRM and weekly sales review
- [ ] Monthly dashboard: sales, margin, yield, debtor days
- [ ] Collect customer feedback; fix issues fast

## After 90 days

- Months 4–6: ISO 9001 certification audit; expand to 2–3 distributors;
  OEM approvals.
- Months 6–12: reach break-even volume; add custom/multi-winding products;
  plan CE certification.
- Year 2: Level B upgrade (more winders, CNC, bigger oven), new product
  families (Module 12).

## Final checklist — are you ready to sell?

- [ ] Every model has: design sheet, BOM, cost sheet, work instruction,
      test limits, inrush rating, catalogue page
- [ ] 100 % routine test with recorded results
- [ ] Type-test reports (or scheduled) for main models
- [ ] Price list, terms of sale, warranty policy
- [ ] Stock of fast-moving models
- [ ] At least 10 qualified customer leads in the pipeline
