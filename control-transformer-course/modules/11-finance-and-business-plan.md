# Module 11 — Finance, Legal & Business Plan

## 11.1 Funding the business

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Owner's capital / family | Banks usually expect promoter contribution (often 10–25 % of project cost) |
| Term loan (bank / NBFC) | For machinery and building; secured on assets |
| Working-capital limit (cash credit / overdraft) | For stock and receivables |
| Government MSME schemes | Many countries offer credit-guarantee, subsidy or interest-subvention schemes for manufacturing MSMEs — check current schemes with your bank or MSME office |
| Equipment leasing / supplier credit | Reduces upfront cash |
| Equity investors | For Level C or fast growth |

## 11.2 Working capital — the silent killer

Working capital = Inventory + Receivables − Payables.

Example (illustrative): monthly sales 20 lakh; customers pay in 60 days;
copper and steel held 30 days; suppliers give 15 days credit:

| Item | Days | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Raw-material stock | 30 days of material cost (~65 % of sales) | ~13 lakh |
| WIP + finished goods | 10 days of cost | ~6 lakh |
| Receivables | 60 days of sales | ~40 lakh |
| Less: payables | 15 days of material | ~(6.5) lakh |
| **Working capital needed** | | **~52 lakh** |

That is more than 2.5 months of sales — often more than the machinery cost.
Plan for it, negotiate payment terms, and collect on time.

## 11.3 Project report (bank format)

Use [`templates/business-plan-outline.md`](../templates/business-plan-outline.md).
Key financial statements to project for 5 years:

1. Project cost & means of finance
2. Production capacity and utilisation (e.g. 40 % → 55 % → 65 % → 75 % …)
3. Sales revenue by product
4. Cost of production (materials, labour, power, overheads)
5. Projected profit & loss
6. Projected balance sheet and cash flow
7. Depreciation and loan repayment schedule
8. Ratios: **DSCR** (debt service coverage, banks look for ≥ 1.5 average),
   current ratio, break-even %, IRR / payback
9. Sensitivity: copper +15 %, sales −10 %, price −5 %

## 11.4 Legal & commercial essentials

- Choose entity type with your accountant (liability, tax, compliance cost).
- Standard **terms & conditions of sale**: prices, taxes, delivery, payment,
  warranty scope and limits, liability cap, jurisdiction.
- Supplier agreements with quality clauses.
- Distributor agreements: territory, targets, pricing, credit, termination.
- Employment contracts and statutory benefits.
- **Product liability insurance**, fire & stock insurance, goods-in-transit.
- Protect IP: trademark the brand; keep design sheets confidential; NDAs with
  OEM customers for custom designs.
