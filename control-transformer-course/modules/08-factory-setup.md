# Module 08 — Factory Setup: Machinery, Layout & Investment

## 8.1 Three ways to start

| Level | Description | Capacity (indicative) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Micro / assembly unit** | Buy stamped laminations & bobbins; manual / semi-auto winding; dip varnish; basic test bench | 50–150 units/day, 25 VA–1 kVA | First-time entrepreneurs, low capital |
| **B — Small manufacturing unit** | Multiple semi-auto + one CNC winder, proper oven, full routine test bench, ISO 9001 | 200–500 units/day, up to 5 kVA | Supplying panel builders & OEMs regionally |
| **C — Integrated unit** | Own lamination press line, multi-spindle CNC winding, VPI, type-test lab, certifications (CE / UL) | 1000+ units/day, plus 3-phase & custom | National brand / export |

Most successful companies **start at Level A, prove the market, then grow
to B**. Don't buy a press line before you have steady orders.

## 8.3 Space and utilities (indicative)

| Item | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area | 1,000–1,500 ft² | 3,000–5,000 ft² | 10,000+ ft² |
| Connected load | ~15–25 kW | 40–80 kW | 150 kW+ |
| Staff | 5–10 | 15–35 | 60+ |

Oven(s) and VPI are the biggest power users — size your sanctioned load
accordingly.

## 8.4 Layout principles

Arrange in **process sequence** to avoid back-tracking:

```
 ┌───────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌───────────┐
 │ RAW STORE │──►│ WINDING  │──►│ CORE     │──►│ VARNISH & │
 │ + incoming│   │ BAY      │   │ ASSEMBLY │   │ OVEN      │
 │ inspection│   └──────────┘   └──────────┘   │ (separate,│
 └───────────┘                                 │ ventilated│
                                               └─────┬─────┘
 ┌───────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐         │
 │ DISPATCH  │◄──│ PACKING  │◄──│ TEST BAY │◄─ FINAL ASSEMBLY
 │ + FG store│   │          │   │ (fenced) │
 └───────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘
```

- Varnish/oven area **separated** for fire safety and fumes.
- HV test bay **fenced and interlocked**.
- Good lighting (≥ 500 lux) at winding stations; ESD/clean benches not
  essential but cleanliness is.
- Keep space for a small office, design desk and sample display room for
  visiting customers.

## 8.5 Investment structure

Your project cost will typically consist of:

| Head | Notes |
|---|---|
| Land & building | Rent first if possible — keeps capital for working capital |
| Plant & machinery | Quote from at least 3 suppliers; include installation |
| Test equipment | Do not economise — quality is your brand |
| Electrical installation | Panel, cabling, earthing pits |
| Furniture, IT, software | |
| Pre-operative expenses | Registrations, design, prototypes, certification tests |
| Contingency | 5–10 % |
| **Margin money for working capital** | Usually the most under-estimated item (see Module 11) |

> Machine and building costs vary widely by country, city and year — use this
> structure with **current local quotations**, not figures from a book.

## 8.6 Make vs buy decisions

| Item | Start | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Laminations | Buy stamped | Own press only at high, steady volume |
| Bobbins | Buy standard | Own moulds for custom designs |
| Brackets | Buy from sheet-metal vendor | Keep outsourced |
| Terminal blocks | Buy | Buy |
| Winding, assembly, test | **Make (core competence)** | Make |
