# Module 07 — Standards, Certification & Compliance

Standards decide your insulation distances, test voltages, temperature limits
and markings. Buy the official copies of the standards you design to — and
check the **current edition**, since standards are revised periodically.

## 7.1 Product standards (commonly applied)

| Standard | Scope | Market |
|---|---|---|
| **IEC 61558-1** | Safety of transformers, reactors, power supply units — general requirements | International / EU |
| **IEC 61558-2-2** | Particular requirements for **control transformers** | International / EU |
| IEC 61558-2-1 / -2-4 / -2-6 | Separating / isolating / safety isolating transformers | International |
| EN 61558 series | European adoption of IEC 61558 (CE, Low Voltage Directive) | EU |
| **IS 12021** | Control transformers for switchgear and controlgear (≤ 1000 V AC) | India |
| UL 5085-1 / -2 (and UL 506) | Low-voltage / specialty transformers | USA |
| CSA C22.2 No. 66 | Specialty transformers | Canada |
| IEC 60204-1 | Electrical equipment of machines — sets rules for control circuits that your customers must follow | Machinery |
| IEC 60085 | Thermal classification of insulation | Reference |
| IEC 60317 | Winding-wire specifications | Reference |

## 7.2 What the standard will specify (read for each)

- Rated values and markings on nameplate
- Test voltages for dielectric strength
- Insulation resistance minimum
- Temperature-rise limits for each insulation class
- Creepage and clearance distances (by working voltage, pollution degree,
  material group)
- Short-circuit and overload protection behaviour
- Mechanical strength, terminal requirements, protection against electric
  shock

## 7.3 Certification / marks

| Mark | How |
|---|---|
| **CE** (EU) | Self-declaration under the Low Voltage Directive (and RoHS), backed by a technical file and test report to EN 61558 — commonly obtained from a third-party lab |
| **UL / cUL / CSA** (North America) | Third-party listing / recognition with factory follow-up inspections |
| **BIS** (India) | Check whether your product falls under any compulsory BIS scheme at the time you start; otherwise IS-compliance + test reports from NABL-accredited labs is usual for tenders |
| **RoHS / REACH** | Material declarations from suppliers (lead-free solder, restricted substances) |
| **ISO 9001** | Quality management certification — often a pre-qualification for OEMs and tenders |

## 7.4 Approvals that win orders

- **Government / PSU / utility vendor registration** — needs type-test reports,
  ISO 9001, financial statements and factory inspection.
- **OEM vendor approval** — customer audits your factory and tests samples;
  once approved you get repeat orders.
- **Consultant approval lists** — EPC contractors and consultants specify
  "approved makes" in project specifications. Getting listed is a major sales
  goal (Module 10).

## 7.5 Business registrations (India example — verify current rules)

| Registration | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Business entity (proprietorship / LLP / Pvt Ltd) | Legal structure |
| **Udyam (MSME) registration** | MSME benefits, priority lending, tender advantages |
| **GST registration** | Tax invoicing; check applicable GST rate for transformers (HSN 8504) |
| Factory licence / shop & establishment | Labour-law compliance depending on workforce size and power use |
| Pollution-control consent | Varnish / solvent use may need consent from the State Pollution Control Board |
| Fire NOC | Varnish and solvent storage |
| Electricity connection (industrial) | Sanctioned load for ovens and machines |
| Trademark | Protect your brand name |
| IEC (Import Export Code) | If you export or import materials |

Other countries have equivalent registrations — consult a local chartered
accountant / consultant.

## 7.6 Compliance calendar

Keep a calendar of renewals: licences, ISO surveillance audits, calibration
due dates, UL follow-up visits, insurance, statutory filings.
