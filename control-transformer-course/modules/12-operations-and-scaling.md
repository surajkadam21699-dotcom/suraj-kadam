# Module 12 — Operations, Scaling & Growth

## 12.1 Daily / weekly operating rhythm

| Frequency | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily (10 min) | Shop-floor huddle: yesterday's output, rejects, today's plan, material shortages |
| Daily | Dispatch plan; test-result review |
| Weekly | Sales pipeline review; production plan for next week; purchase planning |
| Monthly | P&L, KPIs, customer complaints, supplier performance |
| Quarterly | Management review (ISO), customer visits, price-list review |

## 12.2 Production planning

- **Make-to-stock** fast-moving standard models (e.g. 100, 250, 500 VA at
  common voltages) — set min/max stock levels.
- **Make-to-order** custom and large units.
- Batch similar wire sizes together to cut changeover time.
- Varnish/oven is usually the bottleneck — plan batches around oven cycles.

## 12.3 People

- Winding operators: train for 2–4 weeks with work instructions; certify
  skill levels; pay incentive for good output **and** quality.
- Key roles to hire early: production supervisor, quality / test engineer,
  sales engineer, accounts.
- Cross-train so every operation has at least two trained people.

## 12.4 Continuous improvement

- 5S in every area.
- Track first-pass yield; Pareto the top defects monthly; fix root causes.
- Value-engineer designs every year: re-optimise copper/steel as material
  prices change.
- Standardise components across models (fewer lamination sizes, wire sizes,
  bobbins).

## 12.5 Growth paths

| Path | Description |
|---|---|
| **Wider rating range** | Up to 10 kVA single-phase; three-phase control / isolation transformers |
| **Adjacent products** | Isolation transformers, auto-transformers, chokes / reactors, current transformers (CTs), SMPS/24 V DC power supplies (trading or own), servo stabiliser transformers |
| **Custom engineering** | OEM-specific designs — higher margins, sticky customers |
| **Certifications** | CE then UL to unlock export and multinational OEMs |
| **Private label** | Manufacture for brands / distributors |
| **Backward integration** | Lamination stamping, bobbin moulding — at scale only |
| **Automation** | CNC multi-spindle winders, automated test stations with barcode logging |
| **Geographic expansion** | Distributors in new regions, export via B2B platforms |

## 12.6 Risks and mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Copper / steel price spikes | Price-variation clauses, timely buying, design optimisation |
| Customer concentration | No single customer > 25–30 % of sales |
| Bad debts | Credit limits, advance from new customers, credit insurance |
| Quality failure at a key customer | 100 % testing, traceability, fast response |
| Key-person dependency | Documentation, cross-training |
| Low-cost competition | Differentiate on service, speed, certification, engineering support |
| Technology shift (SMPS replacing small transformers) | Focus on inrush-heavy and isolation-critical applications; add power supplies to range |
