# Module 10 — Market, Sales & Marketing

## 10.1 Market demand drivers

Control-transformer demand follows the volume of **control panels** being
built, which tracks:

- Industrial capex (new factories, machine tools, process plants)
- Infrastructure: water & sewage, metro/rail, airports, buildings (HVAC,
  lifts, fire-fighting pumps)
- Agriculture (pump starters) and solar pumping
- Renewable energy and EV charging (auxiliary supplies)
- Replacement / maintenance market

## 10.2 Customer segments

| Segment | Buys | What they value | How to reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Panel builders** (small–large) | Standard models, steady volume | Price, availability, quick delivery | Direct visits, distributors, WhatsApp catalogue |
| **OEMs** (machine builders, HVAC, lifts) | Custom designs, annual contracts | Consistency, approval, engineering support | Direct key-account selling |
| **EPC contractors / consultants** | Specify "approved makes" | Certification, track record | Technical presentations, approvals |
| **Electrical distributors / traders** | Stock standard range | Margin, brand pull, credit | Distributor agreements |
| **Maintenance / MRO** | Replacements | Off-the-shelf availability | Distributors, e-commerce, B2B portals |
| **Government / utilities** | Tendered volumes | Lowest compliant price, approvals | Vendor registration, e-procurement portals |
| **Export** | Standard & private label | CE/UL, price, reliability | B2B portals, trade fairs |

## 10.3 Competitive positioning

Map your local competitors: brand, range, price level, delivery time,
certifications, weaknesses. Common winning angles for a new entrant:

- **Fast delivery** of standard models (stock in hand) and **custom
  designs in days**, not weeks.
- **Published inrush ratings and selection support** (Module 02).
- **Finger-safe terminals, clear tap marking, test certificate with every
  unit** — small things panel builders notice.
- Value pricing with reliable quality.

## 10.4 Sales channels — start-up plan

1. **Direct to panel builders** in your city/region — visit, leave samples,
   follow up weekly. These become your base load.
2. **2–3 distributors** in key industrial areas once you have stock.
3. **OEM key accounts** — target 5 OEMs; customise for them.
4. **Online presence**: website with catalogue + selection guide, Google
   Business profile, B2B marketplaces, LinkedIn page.
5. **Tenders** after ISO 9001 and type-test reports are in hand.

## 10.5 Marketing toolkit

- Product catalogue (PDF + print): ratings, dimensions, inrush table,
  wiring diagrams, ordering code.
- **Ordering code system**, e.g. `CT-0250-415/110-F` = 250 VA, 415→110 V,
  class F.
- Datasheets and installation leaflet.
- Sample kit / cut-section demo unit for visits.
- Case studies and customer testimonials.
- Trade-fair participation (regional electrical expos).
- Digital content: short videos on "how to size a control transformer",
  posted on LinkedIn / YouTube.

## 10.6 Sales process & CRM

```
Lead → Qualify (volume, specs) → Samples / technical offer → Approval → Price
negotiation → First order → Delivery & feedback → Repeat / annual contract
```

Track every lead in a CRM (even a spreadsheet): company, contact, products,
volume potential, stage, next action date. Review weekly.

## 10.7 Customer service

- Reply to enquiries the same day.
- Dispatch standard models within 24–48 hours.
- Clear warranty policy; replace first, investigate later for key customers.
- Visit top customers quarterly.
